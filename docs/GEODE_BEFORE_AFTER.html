2 · GEODE self-correction loop: what its trained geometry questioned

The loop trains on the proposed facts, then asks the trained geometry which facts do not fit. Its duplicate resolution flags a fact as a cap outlier when, among several facts sharing the same head and relation, it sits far outside the relation's learned cap.
